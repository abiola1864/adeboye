About the Review

The Adeboye Review is a platform for clear, evidence-driven writing on governance, leadership, and the institutions that shape public life in Nigeria. It exists to deepen public understanding of how power works, how institutions perform, and what it would take to build systems that serve citizens more effectively.

The platform grew out of a long-standing concern with how Nigeria is governed and a commitment to move beyond headlines toward deeper institutional analysis. It examines the structures, incentives, and leadership cultures that shape national outcomes, with the belief that many of Nigeria’s challenges can be better understood through the strength or weakness of its public institutions.

Our Mission

Our mission is to serve as a public square that promotes accountability, encourages informed civic engagement, and contributes to conversations that strengthen public institutions. This is not a platform for outrage or partisanship. It is grounded in evidence, written with rigour, and committed to thoughtful public analysis.

“Nigeria’s governance journey is a shared story, and so is the work of imagining what it can become.”
About the Founder

Kunle J. Adeboye is a governance and public policy analyst who examines how leadership culture, democratic norms, and institutional performance shape Nigeria’s outcomes — and what reforms are needed to rebuild public trust and strengthen accountability.

He has spent years studying the patterns behind Nigeria’s governance challenges: not only what happened, but why institutions behave as they do, and what it would take to build systems that truly work for Nigerians.

The Adeboye Review is his contribution to public understanding — written for people who care about how power is used, how societies are organised, and how Nigeria can move from potential to performance.

Who This Is For
  • Nigerians at home and across the diaspora
  • Readers who want to understand how societies succeed or fail
  • Researchers, analysts, and practitioners working on governance
  • People who value clarity, evidence, and thoughtful public analysis
